## What the market has converged on (= table stakes)
1. **Autopilot/series posting** — AutoShorts AND Nullface both sell it as the headline. Non-negotiable for us. (Queued: next major milestone.)
2. **Caption style packs** — Crayo's 15 preset caption looks are a cited standout. We have ONE style. Cheap for us (ASS templates) → high visible value.
3. **Language breadth** — 50+ is the advertised norm; we curated 14 voices/4 languages but edge-tts supports 40+ languages free → expand catalog.
4. **Format templates** — Reddit-story / fake-text / split-screen gameplay are proven VIRAL FORMATS (Crayo/Nullface). Different from our narrative styles. Candidates for S3.
5. **A real free tier** — most have one; Crayo's absence is their top complaint. Ours stays.

## What NOBODY on this list has (our moat, confirmed)
- **Live niche trend radar** inside the creation flow (they all start from a blank prompt or canned lists)
- **BYO API keys** (cost weapon)
- **Hindi/India-first** pricing and voices
- **Free script-only mode** (acquisition hook)
- **Monetization loop** (OneFlancer, S3+) — the endgame none of them are building toward
