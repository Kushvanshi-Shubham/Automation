# Funding, competitions and programmes

> Researched 2026-09-08. Deadlines move — verify on the official page before
> applying. Filtered for what **Kliptos can actually enter**: an existing,
> pre-revenue AI product with one founder, India-based, global ambition.
> No student-status routes here — nothing below depends on being enrolled.

## Two filters that decide everything

1. **Does it accept an existing product?** Hackathons do not — they require a
   brand-new build, which is why [[kliptos-hackathon-plan]] was dropped.
   Startup competitions and accelerators are the inverse: they *want* a
   working product. Every entry below passes this filter.
2. **Does it need a registered entity?** Indian programmes almost always want
   Pvt Ltd / LLP / Partnership. Global accelerators do not — YC and a16z
   incorporate you (usually as a Delaware C-corp). **Pre-revenue is fine
   everywhere below.** Unregistered is the only thing that blocks doors.

### The two I would actually spend the evening on

**a16z Speedrun — 25 September.** This is the closest thematic fit of anything
on the page: Speedrun funds games, AI, consumer and *media* tooling, which is
exactly what Kliptos is. Solo founders and non-US founders are explicitly
welcome, and they run a Global Founders Program for visas and banking. The
$10M+ in credits alone would cover Vertex for years — more useful to us right
now than the cash. Acceptance is under 1%, so treat it as a free lottery ticket
with a good story, not a plan.

**Startup League — 15 September, but only if the entity exists.** ₹10 crore
equity-free and it accepts Idea-stage, so the product is more than enough.
Seven days out, and registration is not a seven-day job — realistically this
one is next cycle unless incorporation is already moving.

## Always open — no deadline, no equity

- **OpenVC** — free. 20,000+ verified investors, submit a deck, get open/read
  analytics per investor. The lowest-effort item on this page and the only one
  that gives you *data* on whether the deck is working.
- **NVIDIA Inception** and **Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** — rolling,
  free, non-dilutive credits and cloud. Same category as the Google credit
  already in progress.
- **Product Hunt** — not funding, but the cheapest way to turn "two accounts
  and one render" into real traction, which is what every application above
  actually scores you on.

## Not worth your time

- **Hackathons**, still. Every one found (NexHack, GatewayHacks, InnoVenture,
  IEEEXtreme, United Hacks) requires a new build or is student-only. A win
  credits a weekend demo, not Kliptos.
- **Anything charging an application fee.** None of the above do.
