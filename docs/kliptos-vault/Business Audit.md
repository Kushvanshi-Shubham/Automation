# Business Audit (2026-07-28)

Full report: `../BUSINESS_AUDIT.md`

## Assumptions challenged
1. **"Creators will pay for AI shorts"** — market crowded & churny; moat is trend→monetization loop, not generation. YouTube demonetizes mass-produced synthetic content → don't market "fully autopilot".
2. **"Creators and influencers are one market"** — they're opposites. Brands don't sponsor faceless channels. OneFlancer = second funnel, not a flywheel. → deferred to S4.
3. **"Free tier validates economics"** — free users ≠ payers. Paid tier from day one.
4. **Solo founder scope** — plan spanned 3 companies (video pipeline + marketplace + email infra). → sequenced [[Roadmap]].
5. **India + US together** — yes, but localized pricing (₹499 Pro) + Razorpay/UPI + DPDP compliance.

## Outreach automation: DELETED
Multi-domain rotation/warm-up = spam infrastructure. Legal (CAN-SPAM/GDPR/DPDP) + deliverability + poisons brand side.
Replacement: brands post briefs → creators apply → AI writes the pitch → warm outreach sent from creator's own email. Integrate Instantly/Smartlead later if ever needed.

## SWOT (condensed)
- **S:** differentiation thesis, correct stack, launch-quality UI, honest founder
- **W:** 0% logic built, solo, headline feature underwater as priced ([[Unit Economics]])
- **O:** India creator economy at INR pricing; falling AI video costs; analytics media kits
- **T:** YouTube policy, API dependency, incumbent speed, OAuth verification delay

## Investor verdict
Today: no — pre-product, economics underwater as originally priced.
After S2 gate (30 paying subs, ≥60% margin, >40% M2 retention): fundable seed story, esp. with India traction.
